## Differences and stability

**Run 1:** an extra leading digit appears in the target raw Total and carries into the workbook. The raw target was not marked unclear. The workbook score labels it `wrong_flagged` because the processor adds its general handwriting warning to Total. That warning is not evidence that the numeral error was detected, rejected or corrected; the wrong number remains in Excel.

**Run 2:** the neighbouring Cash Memo raw Total includes a currency prefix omitted by the frozen reference. Its digits and terminal dash agree, and the workbook number is correct. This is the same source-supported formatting difference diagnosed in the earlier 0.3.12 report. Its strict raw mismatch is retained without changing the reference or treating it as a numerical error.

**Run 3:** all configured strict checks pass.

Across the three runs, **2 / 8 configured raw cells differ** (the two Totals), and **1 / 10 configured workbook cells differs** (the target Total). These consistency counts include configured blank cells and are reported separately from the nonblank accuracy denominators. Classifications agree across all three runs. Unscored descriptions, notes and other fields are outside this consistency measurement.

All three command traces read `document-types.md`. Runs 1, 2 and 3 made respectively **2, 2 and 3 image calls**, all returning image content. There were no nonzero shell-command exits. These are observations of tool output, not proof of model attention or an explanation of why one run failed. Different reading paths do not establish a causal relationship with correctness.

## Onboarding and verification

All three new chats asked the policy question once and waited for their own actual owner reply. Before replying, they only read skill instructions; no document work, folder listing or workspace mutation was observed. None repeated the question during extraction. The automatic opening-format audit is **invalid in 3 / 3** because explanatory suffixes and combined instruction reads fall outside its matcher. Separate hash-bound maintainer reviews record the observed pause without replacing that result. A response is recorded, not verified reading or consent.

All **189 unit/regression tests** pass, including fresh direct-private initiation, rejecting an invalid lock before preparation, and refusing a scripted private reply. Package/link checks and whitespace checks pass. Package, dataset, previous reports and reference hashes are unchanged. The final JSON/CSV agree with the archived scorer outputs. No prompt change, commit or push was made during these repeats.

This is three independent session histories on **one reused document page**, not three independent documents or an unseen holdout. The result supports consistent Cash Memo classification within these runs, while directly disproving a claim that the target amount is consistently read correctly by this package. Further prompt work would need a separately versioned change and evaluation; this report preserves the requested unchanged-package test.
