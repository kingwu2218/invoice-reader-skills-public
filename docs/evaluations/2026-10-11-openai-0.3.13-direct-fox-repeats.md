## Reusable prompt change

The [OpenAI entry](../../skills/openai/SKILL.md) now routes handwritten amounts and requested numeric measurements to [handwritten number verification](../../skills/openai/references/handwritten-numbers.md) before accepting them, even if the initial reading seems clear. The new reference requires:

- An upright local enlarged view retaining the label, complete token, units/currency and neighbouring rows/rules. Rotation alone is not the check.
- A fresh local reading and inspection of character boundaries, including whether a stroke continues beyond the writing as a rule or crease. Genuine leading digits must remain; a shorter number is not preferred automatically.
- First/local readings, the view artifact and the visible resolution reason in the reading ledger. Agreement, arithmetic or another labelled amount cannot establish the characters.
- A blank, field-specific unclear result when the complete token remains unresolved. A general handwriting warning cannot justify entering an unresolved number.

There are no expected values, supplier names, fixed coordinates or source-specific recognition rules in this change. Only the OpenAI entry and its new reference change; shared core/runtime and Anthropic 0.3.13 remain unchanged. The scorer and frozen answers are unchanged.

## What the traces show

All three runs read the new reference, created a local target-amount view, requested that view through the image tool and received image content. Maintainer visual review confirms that these views retain the field label, complete token and surrounding rows/rules. All three ledgers record first/local readings and a visible boundary reason. The latter two explicitly distinguish a continuing fold/rule from a leading digit.

**All three recorded initial target readings were already correct.** These traces therefore do not demonstrate a wrong first reading being corrected by the crop, and they do not establish causality. They show that the new procedure was used alongside three correct target readings. Two views read by the same model are not independent verification.

Run 3 treats the crossed leading mark on the neighbouring Total as unresolved currency-versus-digit evidence. It marks Total unclear, leaves it out of raw fields and Excel, and records the reason. The separate line amount remains present; the model does not substitute it for the labelled total. This avoids entering an uncertain number but loses a field whose owner-confirmed reference is readable. Runs 1 and 2 extract it. The existing raw-reference currency-prefix limitation remains unchanged; this evaluation does not resolve every transcription-format convention.

There are **no wrong numeric outputs among the configured scored cells in this sample**, but there is one missing expected amount. Compared with 0.3.12, target correctness rises from 2 / 3 to 3 / 3 while aggregate workbook accuracy stays 20 / 21: an incorrect target value has been replaced, across these samples, by a missing neighbouring value. Whole-page accuracy must not be presented as improved to perfection.

Only the neighbouring Total varies across repeats: **1 / 8 configured raw cells** and **1 / 10 workbook cells**. These consistency counts include configured blanks and are distinct from nonblank accuracy. Unscored notes and fields are outside this measurement.

None repeats the question after the reply. Automatic opening-format audits remain invalid in all three runs; maintainer acceptance of the observed wait does not replace those failures or assert verified policy reading.

All **190 current unit/regression tests**, generated-package/link checks and skill validation pass. The package and source/reference hashes stayed fixed during evaluation; earlier reports remain unchanged. The report matrix agrees with archived scores. No commit or push was performed.

These are three fresh sessions on one reused prompt-development page, not an unseen holdout or a general handwriting-accuracy estimate. Genuine-leading-digit and other document counterexamples were not live-tested on this new package in this request. The prompt has a reusable scope, but its generalization and the added abstention require broader evaluation before promotion.
